## Core rare plant topics

### Variegation and growth
Learn how to distinguish normal variation from reversion, how to read stem and node variegation, and why light can support a variegated plant without restoring a fully green growth point.

### Light and grow lights
Compare natural and artificial light, use plant response rather than vague labels alone, and acclimate rare aroids gradually when increasing intensity.

### Soil, watering and roots
Build an airy root zone, recognize when a mix is staying wet too long, and diagnose root rot by inspecting roots instead of relying on leaf color alone.

### Propagation
Choose viable nodes, compare water and sphagnum rooting, reduce rot risk and wait for a developed root system before potting up.
