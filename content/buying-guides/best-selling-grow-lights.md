## What matters most when choosing a grow light

For most indoor rare-plant owners, the best choice is not automatically the biggest-looking light or the product with the longest feature list. Start with the physical setup you actually have: one large plant, one compact shelf, an existing lamp fixture or several smaller pots.

A bulb-format light is easiest to integrate when you already have a compatible fixture. A clip-on light is usually simpler for one dedicated plant. Multiple compact halo lights are easier to distribute across several smaller pots.

## Before you buy

Check the current product page for dimensions, electrical specifications, customer feedback and availability. Measure the space around your plant first, and make sure the light format can be positioned where you actually need it rather than choosing only from photos.
