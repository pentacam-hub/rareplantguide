## Which one should a Monstera owner choose?

If you already own a good E26 fixture and want the current volume leader in this three-product snapshot, **Briignite** is the straightforward bulb-based choice.

If you want the simplest all-in-one light for one Monstera or one compact plant area, **GooingTop** is the more practical setup because the clamp, flexible neck and timer are already built in.

If you have several smaller rare plants rather than one large plant, **Aokrean** gives you three separate light points in one purchase.
