## Diagnose before you change the care routine

Many rare-plant symptoms overlap. Use the visible symptom as the starting point, inspect the relevant plant part, then change the smallest number of variables needed. The diagnostic pages above are designed to lead into the existing detailed guides rather than repeat them.
