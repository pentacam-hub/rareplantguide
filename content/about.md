The Rare Plant Guide is an independent resource for people growing rare, tropical and variegated houseplants at home.

Our goal is simple: turn confusing plant symptoms and conflicting care advice into practical next steps. The guides focus on the problems collectors face most often—root rot, poor light, pests, unsuitable soil, propagation setbacks and loss of variegation.

## What you will find here

- Step-by-step care and troubleshooting guides
- Clear explanations of why common plant problems happen
- Practical prevention and rescue checklists
- A free [Plant Symptom Checker](/tools/symptom-checker.html) that points you to the most relevant guide

Plant care always depends on the specimen, climate and growing conditions. The information on this site is educational and should be adapted to what you observe in your own plants.
