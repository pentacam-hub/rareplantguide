## Diagnose before you change the care routine

Many rare-plant symptoms overlap. Yellowing can involve roots, watering, light or acclimation; greener variegated growth can involve genetics rather than fertilizer. Use the guides above to gather evidence first, then change the smallest number of variables needed.
