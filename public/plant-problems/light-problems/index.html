Light problem

Does Your Rare Aroid Need More or Less Light?

Diagnose rare aroid light problems from stretched growth, small leaves, bleaching, crisp patches and recent light changes before moving the plant.

Use the Symptom Checker Browse all plant problems
Quick answer

What this symptom can mean

Light problems can point in opposite directions. Long internodes, weak growth and smaller leaves can suggest too little usable light, while bleaching, hot leaf surfaces or crisp patches after a sudden move can suggest too much intensity.

Check first

Look for these clues

  1. Compare internode length and new leaf size with earlier growth.
  2. Look for bleaching, pale patches or crisp damage on the side facing the strongest light.
  3. Note whether the plant was recently moved closer to a window or grow light.
  4. Measure light at leaf height when possible instead of judging by room brightness.
Possible causes

Do not assume one cause

  • Too little usable lightThe plant may stretch, produce smaller leaves or slow down when it cannot capture enough light.
  • Too much light too quicklyA sudden increase can cause bleaching or crisp damage before the plant has acclimated.
  • Another care problemWeak growth can also involve roots, pests or acclimation, so check for additional evidence.
What to do next

Change one thing at a time

1Measure or compare

Use the practical light guide and observe the plant at leaf height rather than relying only on window direction.

2Adjust gradually

Move the plant or fixture in small steps and watch the next growth rather than making a large sudden change.

3Rule out nearby problems

If growth stays weak, inspect roots and pests instead of continuing to increase light.
