Pink Princess Philodendron

Philodendron 'Pink Princess'

A practical Pink Princess Philodendron profile for bright filtered light, loose soil, careful watering, climbing support and pink variegation management.

Difficulty: Intermediate Habit: Climbing / trailing
Quick care

At a glance

LightBright, filtered or indirect light
WaterWater more freely during active growth but avoid waterlogging; reduce frequency when growth slows
HumidityModerate to high
SoilLoose, free-draining houseplant or aroid mix
TemperatureWarm indoor conditions; keep away from cold drafts
PropagationStem-tip or leaf-bud cutting with viable growth tissue
Care priorities

What matters most

  • Give enough filtered light for compact growth without exposing leaves to hot direct sun.
  • Keep the mix free-draining and avoid leaving roots saturated.
  • Provide climbing support if you want larger, more upright mature growth.
Watch for

Common trouble

  • Spider mites or scale insects, especially on stressed plants.
  • Root stress from dense or persistently wet compost.
  • Very low light can lead to weaker, less compact growth.
Variegation note

Pink patterning can vary strongly from leaf to leaf. Judge the active growth point and several leaves together rather than expecting every new leaf to have the same amount of pink.

Best fit for this plant

Pink Princess does best when the basics stay boring and consistent: filtered light, a loose root zone and steady climbing growth. Do not chase pink by making several care changes at once.
