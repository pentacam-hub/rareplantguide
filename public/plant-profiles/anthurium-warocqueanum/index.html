Anthurium plant profile

Anthurium warocqueanum

Anthurium warocqueanum

A practical Anthurium warocqueanum profile for bright indirect light, high humidity, an open epiphytic root mix, careful watering and velvet-leaf protection.

Difficulty: Advanced Habit: Climbing epiphyte
Quick care

At a glance

LightBright, indirect light
WaterWater thoroughly, then let the medium begin to dry; never leave the roots sitting in water
HumidityHigh humidity with airflow
SoilVery open, well-drained epiphytic mix similar to a coarse orchid-style medium
TemperatureWarm indoor conditions; protect from cold
PropagationStem-tip or leaf-bud cuttings
Care priorities

What matters most

  • Use an open root medium that holds some moisture without staying stagnant.
  • Combine high humidity with airflow rather than sealing the plant into constantly wet conditions.
  • Keep light bright but indirect to protect the long velvet leaves.
Watch for

Common trouble

  • Root rot if the medium stays wet or the pot sits in water.
  • Spider mites, mealybugs and scale insects.
  • Mineral buildup can be a problem in hard-water conditions; cleaner water may help sensitive plants.

Best fit for this plant

Anthurium warocqueanum is less forgiving of a stagnant root zone than many common houseplants. Think epiphytic roots, high atmospheric humidity and bright filtered light, not permanently wet compost.

Care reference: Royal Horticultural Society — Anthurium warocqueanum. Indoor conditions vary, so use plant response and root-zone condition alongside any general range.
