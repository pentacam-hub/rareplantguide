Philodendron plant profile

Philodendron melanochrysum

Philodendron melanochrysum

A practical Philodendron melanochrysum profile for bright filtered light, high humidity, well-drained soil, climbing support and velvet-leaf care.

Difficulty: Intermediate to advanced Habit: Climbing
Quick care

At a glance

LightBright, filtered light
WaterWater moderately and let the upper mix begin to dry; reduce watering when growth slows
HumidityHigh humidity is beneficial
SoilHumus-rich but well-drained, airy mix
TemperatureWarm indoor conditions; protect from temperatures below about 15°C
PropagationStem-tip or leaf-bud cuttings
Care priorities

What matters most

  • Use a vertical support so the climbing plant can mature and size up its velvet leaves.
  • Keep the root mix airy while maintaining steady moisture rather than repeated saturation.
  • Provide bright filtered light without hot direct exposure on velvet foliage.
Watch for

Common trouble

  • Spider mites, mealybugs or scale on stressed foliage.
  • Small or weak new leaves when light, support or root health is inadequate.
  • Root problems in dense, persistently wet compost.

Best fit for this plant

Melanochrysum is a climber, so support is part of care rather than decoration. Stable filtered light, an airy root zone, humidity and something to climb produce a much better result than chasing a perfect watering schedule.

Care reference: Royal Horticultural Society — Philodendron melanochrysum. Indoor conditions vary, so use plant response and root-zone condition alongside any general range.
